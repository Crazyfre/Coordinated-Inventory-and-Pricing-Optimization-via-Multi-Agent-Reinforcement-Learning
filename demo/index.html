A foundation for learning.

Explore how prices, stock and delivery delays affect a store with 10 products. The simulation runs our implemented modules, with demand calibrated on Walmart M5 data.

Completed: foundation · inventory · environment · demand
1

Set a price

Higher prices change the margin and reduce expected demand. Prices are held between weekly decision points.

2

Restock the shelves

Orders are placed daily and arrive after a random delay. All products compete for the same storage space.

3

Measure the outcome

Customers buy what is available. Track economic reward, demand served and sales lost when stock runs out.
